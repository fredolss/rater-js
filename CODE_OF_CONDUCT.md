# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes issues, pull requests, and discussion from anyone.
To keep it a good place to collaborate:

- Be respectful and constructive in comments, issues, and reviews.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Stay on topic — this repo is for `rater-js` the library.

If someone's behavior in this project's spaces is a problem, report it to
the maintainer at fredrik.olsson2@outlook.com. Reports will be handled
privately, and maintainers may edit, lock, or remove content and block
participants who don't follow this.
